# Contributors

Hurtip is built and improved with the help of the community.

We appreciate everyone who contributes their time, knowledge, and ideas to the
project.

## Community Contributors

People who help improve Hurtip through bug reports, pull requests, testing,
suggestions, and feedback.

- **[@J9OOOO](https://github.com/J9OOOO)** — Reported bugs and contributed to
  identifying and investigating floating-point output precision issues.

- **[@J9OOOO](https://github.com/J9OOOO)** — Reported an issue with array index
  assignments and proposed an approach for dynamic array expansion. This
  contribution helped identify the limitation and shape the implementation of

## Acknowledgments
